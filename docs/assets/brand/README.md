# 亚特雷亚助手品牌素材

主标志以“羽翼数字 2”为主题，根据维护者提供的轮廓参考重新设计。青碧色主体、淡金边缘和收束的羽翼，将游戏气质与软件图标的可辨识度结合。

- `atreia-mark.png`：1280 像素透明底原图。
- `atreia-icon.png`：128 像素官网导航与 favicon。
- `atreia-emblem.png`：256 像素首屏与深色背景使用的标志。
- 软件图标位于 `desktop/src-tauri/icons/`，由 Tauri icon 命令从透明原图生成。
- 安装器 welcome/completion 配图为 `installer-dialog.bmp`，493×312；其中左侧 164 像素为游戏场景，右侧留白用于安装文字。
- 安装器顶部配图为 `installer-banner.bmp`，493×58。

素材使用内置图像生成工具创建，官网使用原创云海浮岛场景；汉化功能展示图从本项目的 WebView 前端截图，统计面板明确标注演示数据。

视觉参考：[AION 2 官网](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/index?redirect=false) 的全幅世界场景、栏目导航与游戏品牌呈现方式。

## 图像生成提示词

Logo：Create one original standalone software logo mark for Atreia Assistant. Use the reference's curved numeral 2 and swept feather silhouette as inspiration, redesigned into a professional emblem. A clearly readable numeral 2, restrained feather forms, refined teal and turquoise with a subtle warm platinum edge, crisp silhouette, slight dimensional material shading, excellent small-size legibility. One centered mark on a genuinely transparent background; no text, enclosing tile, frame, comparison sheet or watermark.

首屏：A restrained cinematic fantasy landscape with an ancient pale stone citadel and slender tower on a floating island above a white cloud sea. Detailed original matte painting, silver stone, emerald foliage, turquoise accents, soft morning light. Quiet pale upper sky, low horizon, citadel on the right, no characters, text, game logo or decorative orbs.

安装器：Composite the citadel environment with the feather-numeral 2 logo into a 493×312 welcome-dialog image. Artwork occupies only the left third: a portrait floating-island tower and cloud sea, logo near the upper center. The right two thirds remain solid pure white for the installer's native text. No additional text, controls, watermark or mockup window.
